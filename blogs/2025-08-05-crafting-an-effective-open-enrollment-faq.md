As open enrollment season approaches, HR teams often face an influx of employee questions. From confusion about new plan options to uncertainty about deadlines, these questions can quickly become overwhelming for HR and benefits managers. An effective Open Enrollment FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) can be one of the most powerful tools to streamline communication, reduce repetitive inquiries, and help employees feel informed and confident in their benefits decisions.
