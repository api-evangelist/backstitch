Today, total rewards professionals must design and manage compensation, benefits, and rewards and recognition programs. They do this to attract and retain talent, while also juggling career development tracks that unlock the ability of their employees. However, as pay equity laws evolve, and emerging technologies transform HR, these professionals must constantly update their skills and learn new tools.
